Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Account data: When you create an account, we collect your username and optionally your email address. Passwords are stored as salted hashes and are never readable.

Usage data: We store your watchlist symbols, portfolio holdings, alert thresholds, and preferences to provide the Service.

Session data: A session cookie is used to keep you logged in. No third-party tracking cookies are used.

2. How We Use Data

Your data is used solely to operate the Service: authenticate you, display your watchlist and portfolio, and send password reset emails if configured.

3. Data Storage

Data is stored in a SQLite database on our hosting provider's infrastructure (Railway). We implement reasonable security measures including password hashing and rate limiting on auth endpoints.

4. Third-Party Services

Alpaca Markets: Market data is fetched from Alpaca using your API credentials. Alpaca receives the symbol queries you make.

Railway: The Service is hosted on Railway's infrastructure. Railway has access to the server and database for operational purposes.

Resend (optional): If configured, email addresses are shared with Resend to send password reset emails.

Sentry (optional): If configured, error reports may be sent to Sentry for debugging.

Plausible (optional): If configured, anonymized usage analytics are collected via Plausible.

5. Data Retention

Account data is retained until you request deletion. Contact the account owner to delete your account and associated data.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting the account owner.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
